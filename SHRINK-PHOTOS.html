Shrink photos

Phone photos are 4–10 MB each and make the website slow. This tool makes every photo in your website folder web-sized (about 200–400 KB) while keeping the same names and folders. They look the same on screen.

  1. Optional but wise: keep a copy of your dolkar-site folder as a backup (the originals get replaced).
  2. Click the button and choose your dolkar-site folder, the one with index.html inside.
  3. If the browser asks for permission to view and then edit files, click Allow / Save changes.

This browser can't do this. Please open this file in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a laptop or desktop.

    Not part of the website

    These are inside your dolkar-site folder but the website doesn't use them, so they only make the upload bigger. Move them somewhere else (e.g. your Desktop) before uploading to Netlify.

      iPhone photos that websites can't show

      These are in .HEIC format. Websites can't display them. Convert them to JPG (e.g. drop them on squoosh.app, or on iPhone: Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible) and put the JPG back with the same number.
