Setting up the invitations

Investors get the invitation in their email and answer from their email. Each button opens a ready-made reply, they press Send, and the system reads it. No website needed. Follow the steps in order and tap Mark done as you go; this page remembers where you were.

Part C Your dashboard link 5 min

  1. Your private dashboard. In Apps Script, click the blue Deploy button (top right) → New deployment.

    • Click the ⚙ gear next to "Select type" → Web app
    • Description: Dashboard
    • Execute as: Me (info@jupitertexas.com)
    • Who has access: Only myself
    • Click Deploy

    Copy the Web app link, open it in a new tab in the info@ window, and bookmark it (Cmd + D). That's your dashboard.

  2. Open tracking: lets the dashboard show who opened the email. Do Deploy → New deployment again:

    • ⚙ gear → Web app
    • Description: Tracking
    • Execute as: Me
    • Who has access: Anyone
    • Deploy

    Copy that Web app link. In the sheet, open the Settings tab and paste it into cell B2 (the yellow cell).

    No "Anyone" option, or want to skip this? Leave B2 empty. Everything else still works: you just won't see "Opened", only replies.

Part D Test it on yourself 10 min

  1. Open your dashboard bookmark. Click Send invitations → Send test to me.

    In info@'s Gmail, open the test invitation. Tap Just me. A reply opens with everything filled in. Press Send.

    Within 5 minutes you should get a reply saying "Thank you, Test! You're confirmed…". The test saves nothing in the sheet.

  2. Real test. In the Guests tab, on row 2, type your name, your personal email (not info@), and 0 as the phase.

    Refresh the dashboard → Send invitations → choose Phase 0 → Send 1 invitation.

  3. On your phone, open the email in your personal inbox. Tap Me +1 and press Send. Within 5 minutes check that:

    • You got a thank-you reply in the same email thread
    • A calendar invite from info@ arrived
    • After a refresh, the dashboard shows you as Attending, party size 2

    Then reply to the thank-you with just the word No. Within 5 minutes the dashboard should show Can't make it and the calendar event should disappear.

    All good? Delete your test row (right-click row 2 → Delete row).

Part E Send to investors

  1. In the Guests tab, paste your investors starting at cell A2:

    • Column A: Name (e.g. Raj Mehta)
    • Column B: Email
    • Column C: Phase (1, 2, 3…)

    From Excel: select those three columns of data (no header row), copy, click A2 in the sheet, paste. Leave the grey columns empty; the system fills them.

  2. Dashboard → Send invitations → choose Phase 1. It shows how many it found and how many it will send. Click Send and keep the tab open until it says Done.

    Repeat for each phase when you're ready. Nobody gets the invite twice.

  3. After that, it runs itself.

    • Every 5 minutes it reads RSVP replies, updates the dashboard, sends the calendar invite and a thank-you
    • Replies it can't understand (like "What's the dress code?") stay unread in info@'s inbox for you to answer
    • Reminder emails go out 7, 3 and 1 day before, to everyone who said Yes or Maybe
    • 4 or more days after a phase goes out, use Nudge non-responders on the dashboard to follow up once
    • Every reply is listed in the sheet's Reply log tab

Part F One request to Jupiter's IT person

  1. This helps keep invitations out of spam. Send this to whoever manages the jupitertexas.com domain:

    Hi, could you add one DNS record to jupitertexas.com? We're sending event invitations from info@ and this helps them reach inboxes. Type: TXT Name / Host: _dmarc Value: v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:info@jupitertexas.com Thanks!

If something goes wrong

The dropdown only shows "myFunction"

The code isn't saved. Press Cmd + S (Windows: Ctrl + S), wait until "Unsaved changes" disappears, then open the dropdown again.

Someone replied but the dashboard didn't change

Wait 5 minutes and refresh. Then check the sheet's Reply log tab: it says how each reply was read. "No matching investor" means they replied from an email that isn't on the list and changed the subject; update their row by hand.

Tapping a button doesn't open a reply on a computer

Some computers have no email app set up for these buttons. On a phone it always works. On a computer the investor can simply reply to the invitation with "Yes, 2 of us", "Maybe" or "No"; the system reads those too.

I changed the code and nothing changed in the dashboard

Apps Script: Deploy → Manage deployments. For each deployment: click ✏️ → Version: New version → Deploy. The links stay the same.

The emails land in Spam

Do Part F (DMARC). Also ask your test recipients to move the email to the inbox and mark it "Not spam"; it helps the next ones.
