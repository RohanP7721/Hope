Setting up the invitations

Investors get the invitation in their email and answer with one tap: Just me, Me +1, Me +2, Me +3 or more, Maybe or Can't make it. A short "You're in!" confirmation opens, the answer lands on your dashboard, and a calendar invite follows. Nothing for you to host. Follow the steps in order and tap Mark done as you go; this page remembers where you were.

    Buttons open a reply email instead? The Tracking link isn't in Settings B2 yet, or this email was sent before you added it. Fix B2, then send a new test.

  2. Real test. In the Guests tab, on row 2, type your name, your personal email (not info@), and 0 as the phase.

    Refresh the dashboard → Send invitations → choose Phase 0 → Send 1 invitation.

  3. On your phone, open the email in your personal inbox. Check that:

    • After a refresh, the dashboard shows you as Opened (with pictures shown in the email)
    • Tapping Me +1 opens "You're in" and the dashboard shows Attending, party size 2

    Then tap Can't make it at the bottom of that page. The dashboard should show Can't make it, and the calendar event should disappear.

Part E Send to investors

  1. In the Guests tab, paste your investors starting at cell A2:

    • Column A: Name (e.g. Raj Mehta)
    • Column B: Email
    • Column C: Phase (1, 2, 3…)

    From Excel: select those three columns of data (no header row), copy, click A2 in the sheet, paste. Leave the grey columns empty; the system fills them.

  2. Dashboard → Send invitations → choose Phase 1. It shows how many it found and how many it will send. Click Send and keep the tab open until it says Done.

    Repeat for each phase when you're ready. Nobody gets the invite twice.

  3. After that, it runs itself.

    • Taps show up on the dashboard instantly, and the calendar invite goes out straight away
    • If someone replies by email instead ("Yes, 3 of us"), it's read within 5 minutes. Anything else (like "What's the dress code?") stays unread in info@'s inbox for you to answer
    • Reminder emails go out 7, 3 and 1 day before, to everyone who said Yes or Maybe
    • 4 or more days after a phase goes out, use Nudge non-responders on the dashboard to follow up once
    • Every reply is listed in the sheet's Reply log tab

Part F One request to Jupiter's IT person

  1. This helps keep invitations out of spam. Send this to whoever manages the jupitertexas.com domain:

    Hi, could you add one DNS record to jupitertexas.com? We're sending event invitations from info@ and this helps them reach inboxes. Type: TXT Name / Host: _dmarc Value: v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:info@jupitertexas.com Thanks!

If something goes wrong

The dropdown only shows "myFunction"

The code isn't saved. Press Cmd + S (Windows: Ctrl + S), wait until "Unsaved changes" disappears, then open the dropdown again.

"Opened" never shows up

Check three things. 1) Settings B2 has the Tracking link (ends in /exec). 2) In Deploy → Manage deployments, Tracking says "Anyone". 3) The email was sent after B2 was filled; older emails don't have tracking. Also, many people's email apps hide pictures until they tap "Show images"; those opens only appear once they do, or once they tap a button.

There's no "Anyone" option

Send this to Jupiter's Google Workspace admin:

Hi, could you allow Apps Script web apps to be shared with anyone? In the Google Admin console: Apps → Google Workspace → Drive and Docs → Sharing settings → Sharing options → allow sharing outside jupitertexas.com. It's for the holiday party RSVP buttons. Thanks!
Someone replied but the dashboard didn't change

Wait 5 minutes and refresh. Then check the sheet's Reply log tab: it says how each reply was read. "No matching investor" means they replied from an email that isn't on the list and changed the subject; update their row by hand.

I changed the code and nothing changed

Do Part C, step 9 (update both deployments to a New version).

The emails land in Spam

Do Part F (DMARC). Also ask your test recipients to move the email to the inbox and mark it "Not spam"; it helps the next ones.
